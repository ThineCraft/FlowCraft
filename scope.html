ThineCraft project scope

FlowCraft

Status: Placeholder project. This document describes intended direction; it does not claim that working functionality exists.

Project website

Purpose

FlowCraft is the planned open workflow and integration platform for connecting ThineCraft services and external systems through automation that people can inspect, explain, and operate.

Confirmed direction

  • Readable visual workflows with triggers, branches, decisions, approvals, actions, retries, and outcomes.
  • Open connector contracts for ThineCraft services and external systems.
  • Inspectable data mappings and transformations.
  • Versioned workflow definitions, run history, errors, evidence, and human interventions.
  • Portable automation definitions that can be reviewed and supported outside a closed platform.

Boundaries and non-goals

  • FlowCraft should make a process visible before making it automatic.
  • Opaque background behavior without inspectable definitions or evidence conflicts with the product direction.
  • Domain records remain owned by their source services; FlowCraft coordinates them.
  • No implementation, connector catalog, execution guarantee, or production readiness is currently promised.

Expected integrations

  • ShareCraft content, lists, forms, permissions, and public submission processes.
  • DriveCraft synchronization and migration events.
  • TeamCraft notifications, approvals, and human participation.
  • GanttCraft tasks and schedule changes.
  • FormCraft submissions and PdfCraft document processes.
  • External systems through documented connector contracts.

Open decisions

  • Workflow definition format, expression language, and visual-editor model.
  • Execution, scheduling, queuing, retries, idempotency, compensation, and recovery.
  • Connector security, secrets, delegated identity, permissions, and isolation.
  • Versioning, deployment, environments, testing, observability, and audit retention.

Guidance for AI contributors

  1. Favor inspectable, explainable, and portable automation.
  2. Do not describe planned workflows or connectors as implemented.
  3. Preserve run evidence, error visibility, and human recovery paths in design proposals.
  4. Do not make FlowCraft the owner of records that belong to another service.
  5. Record new product decisions in this file before presenting them as settled scope.
